How an article gets flagged. Two signals: vocabulary drift, the over-use of words that became far more common in biomedical writing after ChatGPT's release (Matsui, 2025, DOI 10.5334/pme.1929), and literal leftover chatbot phrases. No AI-detector service is used.

Strong signal = a chatbot phrase, or an elevated vocabulary score (three or more strongly associated words at high density). Weak signal = a moderate vocabulary score (at least two such words). The percentage on each card is the share of the text made up of words from the AI-associated word list. It is not the probability, or the share of the text, written by AI. This is weak, population-level evidence and never proof that a text was written by AI.

Which model? This page does not try to say which AI tool wrote a text. No available method does that reliably.
